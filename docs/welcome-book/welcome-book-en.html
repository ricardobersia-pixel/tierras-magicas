Welcome!

Tierras Mágicas is a mountain retreat between Villa Pehuenia and Moquehue, surrounded by forest and overlooking the lake. Here the idea is to slow down: rest, spend time together and enjoy the mountains at a different pace.

This guide has everything you need for your stay. If something is missing, message us: we are here to help.

Included in your stay

  • Hot tub (jacuzzi) and sauna
  • Kayaks, stand-up paddleboards, airtrack and floating island
  • Pellet heating (pellets included)
  • Starlink satellite internet with mesh network and 43" Smart TV
  • Bed linen, towels and bathroom amenities
  • Kitchen basics: coffee, tea, sugar, salt, oil and vinegar

Firewood for the fire pit and grill is not included. You can order it on WhatsApp.

When you arrive

  • Park in your cabin’s designated spot.
  • Our receptionist welcomes you, hands over the keys and shows you the cabin, the pellet stove, the hot tub and the sauna.

Before you travel

  • The last few kilometres are on a gravel road: drive slowly.
  • In winter there may be snow and ice. Bring snow chains and check road conditions (see Getting around).
  • Mobile signal is limited. The cabin has Starlink Wi-Fi.
  • Fill up beforehand: there are no petrol stations on the gravel stretches.

Getting here

We are in El Verde, Moquehue (Neuquén, Argentine Patagonia), on Provincial Route 11, facing the El Verde stream. Lake Moquehue is about 50 metres away, along a small path through a forest of ñire and lenga trees (Patagonian beeches). We look out over the Andes, on the border with Chile, and the El Verde stream.

Address: Ruta Provincial 11, Km 8.2, El Verde, Moquehue (CP 8345)

High-speed Starlink satellite internet with a mesh network across the whole property. Good for messaging, browsing, streaming and remote work. During heavy snowstorms it may drop out intermittently.

Heating

The stoves burn pellets, are digital and are controlled over Wi-Fi. Pellets are included: if they are running low, let us know.

  • Don’t block the air outlet or put clothes on top.
  • If the carbon monoxide alarm goes off: open the windows, leave the cabin and call us.

TV and kitchen

There is a 43" Smart TV with streaming (sign in with your own accounts and sign out when you leave). The kitchen has a gas cooker with oven, microwave, fridge-freezer and utensils.

Hot tub and sauna

Both are included in the rate. When demand is high they are used in time slots, booked on WhatsApp.

What we have

  • Hot tub (jacuzzi): for 6 people, water up to 40 °C. In summer, bubble bath and bathrobes included.
  • Cold plunge tub: for 2 people.
  • Sauna: for 6 people, with essential oils and bathrobes.

Opening hours

  • Hot tub: 10:00 am to 10:00 pm, 2-hour slots.
  • Sauna: 10:00 am to 10:00 pm, 1-hour slots.

Let us know in advance: the hot tub takes about 5 hours to heat up and the sauna about 3.

Enjoy them safely

  • Shower before getting in and don’t bring glass containers.
  • Don’t use them after drinking alcohol or for longer than recommended.
  • Children must always be accompanied by an adult.

House rules

  1. Quiet time. Please keep quiet from midnight. There are several cabins, so let’s respect everyone’s peace.
  2. No smoking inside the cabin.
  3. Pets. Medium-sized pets are welcome if you tell us when booking. They must not be left alone in the cabin.
  4. Visitors. The facilities are for guests only. Please ask us before inviting anyone.
  5. Fire. Use only the designated fire pit and grills. During fire season, follow any bans in force. Never leave a fire burning.
  6. Looking after the cabin. Take care of the furniture, equipment and linen, and don’t take them out of the cabin.
  7. Shared spaces. When demand is high, the hot tub, sauna and water equipment are used in time slots.
  8. Rubbish and driving. Sort your rubbish into the bins provided and drive slowly on the property.
  9. When you leave. Return the keys and check you haven’t left anything behind.

FAQ

How do I use the hot tub and sauna?

Included in the rate. Let us know in advance, as they take hours to heat up. Time slots apply when demand is high (page 7).

Is there parking?

Yes, on the property. Park in your cabin’s spot.

Can I use the fire pit and grill?

Yes. Firewood is not included: order it on WhatsApp. When you finish, make sure the fire is out.

We are a bigger group. Is there room?

Up to 4 more people can sleep in the “dormi”, a separate small cabin that shares the bathroom of the rented cabin. Subject to availability, with a per-person charge.

Are you on the lakeshore?

We are about 50 metres from Lake Moquehue, along a path through the ñire and lenga forest, with views of the Andes and the El Verde stream.

I need help. What should I do?

Message us on WhatsApp at any time. For emergencies, see page 16.
